## Technical Highlights

- **Marker recognition and precise alignment.** HSV color preprocessing and a custom ArUco dictionary identify ore blocks and exchange markers. PnP estimates marker poses from their corners; visual pose errors drive PID alignment for the final approach and pickup.
- **LiDAR-inertial localization.** IMU motion prediction and LiDAR scan correction support map-based localization. The technical report describes point-to-map-line residuals for fusion, providing the robot pose used by navigation.
- **Global planning and chassis control.** A* with diagonal moves and turn costs searches for a route. Floyd path simplification and circular-arc corner smoothing reduce unnecessary turns; speed and acceleration constraints produce a trapezoidal velocity profile, followed by Pure Pursuit tracking. Obstacle checks trigger replanning around non-target ore blocks.
- **Collection and placement workflow.** Sequential task logic connects exchange-label recognition, search, approach, alignment, grasping, return, and placement. Target memory supports handling non-target blocks, while lower-layer markers guide alignment for stacked placement. The ROS implementation coordinates task stages through behavior-tree nodes and shared state.
