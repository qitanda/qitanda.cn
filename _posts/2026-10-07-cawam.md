**J Zhu**, Y Xie, Y Wu, Y Cao, H Chen

**Under review** · [Project website](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/7D35/index.html) · [Paper]({{ '/files/CA-WAM/paper.pdf' | relative_url }}) · [Demo video](#demo-video)

CA-WAM learns **contact-aware world representations** for general and contact-rich robot manipulation. It jointly predicts future video, three-dimensional end-effector forces, and actions, using future forces as supervision to capture interaction dynamics that can be difficult to distinguish from images alone. **Measured force is not required at deployment.**

## Overview

![CA-WAM architecture with future force supervision, training-only geometric alignment, and optional dynamic replanning]({{ '/images/projects/CA-WAM/method.png' | relative_url }})

- **Future force supervision.** Visual and force tokens share a world expert, providing physical supervision through joint video, force, and action learning.
- **Training-only geometric alignment.** A frozen VGGT teacher aligns visual representations with multi-view geometry; the teacher is not needed at deployment.
- **Efficient inference and optional replanning.** Actions use current visual representations without generating future video. Predicted force changes can shorten the action execution horizon near anticipated contact transitions.

## Results

Evaluated across **90 simulated tasks** (40 LIBERO and 50 Meta-World tasks) and **five real-world tasks** covering object collection, plug insertion, board wiping, and screw tightening.

| Evaluation | Fast-WAM | CA-WAM |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| LIBERO average success | 97.6% | **98.5%** |
| Meta-World average success | 92.5% | **94.9%** |
| Real-world average success | 52.0% | **71.0%** |

CA-WAM improves overall real-world success by **19 percentage points** over Fast-WAM. On the three contact-rich tasks, average success rises from **35.0% to 61.7%**. Real-world evaluation uses 20 trials per task per method; simulation averages follow the suites and difficulty tiers reported in the paper.

## Details

CA-WAM jointly optimizes future video, force, and action flows with conditional flow matching. An asymmetric attention mask supports efficient action inference. Optional dynamic replanning uses changes in predicted force to decide when to incorporate fresh observations; it adjusts the execution horizon rather than directly regulating interaction force.
