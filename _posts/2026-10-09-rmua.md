**Second Prize, 2023.** Developed an autonomous UAV system for perceiving circular gates and navigating a course with static and moving rings. The project connects visual perception, navigation targets, trajectory generation, and flight control in a ROS-based workflow.

## Technical Highlights

- **Ring detection and learned stereo estimation.** Color segmentation and AAMED ellipse detection identify circular gates. A neural stereo-matching model estimates dense disparity from the left and right camera images; disparity encodes scene depth through stereo geometry. TensorRT FP16 inference accelerates the network for the perception pipeline.
- **Minimum-snap planning and trajectory tracking.** Polynomial trajectories connect navigation waypoints while minimizing squared snap, the fourth derivative of position, to produce smooth flight references. Velocity and acceleration limits guide trajectory generation. Position, velocity, acceleration, and jerk references feed the flight controller, which uses feedback, rotor-drag compensation, and thrust-model estimation to track the trajectory.
- **Visual-inertial estimation and ROS integration.** VINS-Mono combines camera observations with IMU measurements for motion estimation. ROS connects image streams, odometry, perception, planning, and flight commands, with AirSim and PX4/MAVROS interfaces supporting system integration and simulation.
